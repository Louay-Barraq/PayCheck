Account Deletion

Last updated: August 29, 2026

You can request deletion of your PayCheck account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the application installed.

1. Delete Your Account From Within the App

If you have the app installed, this is the fastest way to delete your account:

  • Open PayCheck and sign in
  • Go to Settings
  • Tap Delete my account and confirm

This immediately and permanently erases your account and all associated data from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

2. Request Deletion Without the App

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion by contacting us directly:

Email: REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL
Please send your request from the email address associated with your PayCheck account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", so we can verify and process it.

Requests submitted this way are processed within 30 days.

3. What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account information (email, authentication identifier, display name)
  • Your profile and onboarding preferences (profession, age range, country, currency)
  • All clients and their contract details
  • All payment records and receipt statuses associated with your account

None of this data is retained after deletion, and none of it is shared with or sold to any third party at any point, whether before or after your deletion request.

4. Questions

For any questions about this process, see our full Privacy Policy, or contact us via the email above.
